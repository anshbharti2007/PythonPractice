### Part 1: The Daily Blueprint (The 90/20 Protocol)

To hit a 9+ CGPA, you need approximately 6 hours of high-yield deep work per day. You will run four 90-minute blocks, separated by strict breaks to reset your focus.

**Morning: The Heavy Lift (Math Focus)**

* **08:00 AM – Block 1 (90 mins):** New Concept Acquisition. Read the textbook (*Introduction to Linear Algebra* or IITM theory) or watch the lecture. Do not touch assignments yet.
* **09:30 AM – Break (20 mins):** Screen off. Stand up, stretch, or grab a quick breakfast (e.g., oats and eggs).
* **09:50 AM – Block 2 (90 mins):** Problem-Solving Gauntlet. Apply the **Copy-Close-Solve** loop to the concepts you just read. Copy a worked example, close the book, re-derive it from memory, then tackle 3-5 unsolved problems.
* **11:20 AM – Long Recovery (2-3 hours):** Complete mental disconnect. This is your window to hit the heavy resistance bands and get your protein in (chicken/soya).

**Afternoon/Evening: Secondary Subjects & Code**

* **02:30 PM – Block 3 (90 mins):** Stats/CT/Python. For coding, boot up the Asus TUF, leverage those linear switches, and run through your HackerRank or pseudocode logic loops.
* **04:00 PM – Break (20 mins):** Active recovery.
* **04:20 PM – Block 4 (90 mins):** Assignment Execution & Triage. Finalize IITM graded assignments. If you get stuck, apply the 10-minute solo struggle rule.

### Part 2: The Core Systems

These are the non-negotiable rules for executing the plan.

* **The "Copy-Close-Solve" Loop:** When doing math or stats, copy the textbook's solution line-by-line. Close the book for 5 minutes. Return to a blank page and re-derive the solution from memory. Where you freeze is your actual failure point.
* **The 48-Hour Triage (Contingency Plan):** If Quiz 1 results drop below your 9-point target, pause all forward momentum. Spend 48 hours dissecting the Quiz. Categorize every lost mark:
* *Calculation Error:* You dropped a negative sign. Fix: Slow down in Block 2.
* *Conceptual Error:* You didn't know the setup. Fix: Pause Stats/CT for two days and funnel all 6 daily hours into re-learning that specific math concept.


* **Shelve the ML Book:** *Mathematics for Machine Learning* stays closed until January 2027. Your entire algebraic focus is on Gilbert Strang.
